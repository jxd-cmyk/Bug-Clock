## A transparent desktop clock with 24 bugs by  [Jaroslav M. Soukup](mailto:jsoukup1@centrum.cz). Pick your favourite bug, customise the clock and keep the time in view.

Give the time on your desktop a personal touch. Bug Clock combines a digital clock with 24 bug images by Jaroslav M. Soukup. It displays the current time, including seconds, and the day of the week on a transparent background.
Choose one bug from the catalogue or let all 24 appear in sequence. Set the interval from 10 seconds to 60 minutes. Customise the clock with five sizes, three text colours and a choice of nine fonts. If a selected font is not installed, the app uses a fallback font.

<img width="3440" height="1440" alt="Bug Clock (3)" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/cbc5ce7d-c5b9-4880-ada8-5e505ad3fe70" />

Drag the clock to your preferred position, lock it in place and choose whether it stays above other windows. Right-click the clock or its Windows notification area icon to access the settings. Your preferences are saved locally on your computer.

Bug Clock works offline, requires no account and contains no ads. The app interface is in English. The displayed time follows your computer's system clock.

# Features — one item per field

- Transparent digital clock displaying HH:MM:SS and the day of the week.
- 24 bug images by Jaroslav M. Soukup, with a catalogue for selection.
- Keep one bug or cycle through all 24 at seven intervals from 10 seconds to 60 minutes.
- Five clock sizes and white, black or green text.
- Choice of nine fonts, with a fallback if the selected font is not installed.
- Drag to reposition, lock the position and optionally keep the clock above other windows.
- Access settings from the right-click menu or Windows notification area icon.
- Locally saved preferences; no internet connection, account or ads required.
